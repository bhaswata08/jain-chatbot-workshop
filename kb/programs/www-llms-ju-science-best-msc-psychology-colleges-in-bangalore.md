FAQ's:
Q1. Is Psychology difficult to study?
A1: Psychology can be challenging to study due to its workload and vast content. Students need to develop strong study habits, a distraction-free study space and effective time management to succeed.

Q2. Do Psychologists get paid well?
A2: Yes. Psychologists can be well-paid, but salaries vary based on industry, specialisation, and experience. Fields like neuropsychology and organisational Psychology offer higher salaries compared to other specialisations.

Q3. Does Psychology have a future?
A3: Yes. Psychology has a promising future with a growing demand and diverse career opportunities. Psychology courses equip students with versatile skills applicable in various career fields, primarily healthcare, education, business and law.

Q4. Is Psychology a high paying job?
A4: Some of the highest-paying jobs are Industrial-Organisational (I-O) psychologist, clinical psychologist, sports psychologist, and neuropsychologist.

Q5. Does Psychology need NEET?
A5: NEET is an entrance exam for medical and dental courses in India. Therefore, students need not appear for NEET to enrol in a Psychology programme.

Q6. Is a Psychologist a good career?
A6: Psychology is quite a noble profession, with good earning prospects, especially if students work in urban areas or private practice.

Q7. What are the four main types of Psychology?
A7: The four main types of Psychology are clinical, cognitive, behavioural, and biopsychology.
