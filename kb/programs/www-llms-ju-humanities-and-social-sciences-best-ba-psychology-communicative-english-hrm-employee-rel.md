FAQ's:
Q1: What is Communicative English?
A1: Communicative English is a course that helps students improve their communication skills. It includes reading, writing, speaking, and listening exercises and helps students build confidence.

Q2: What is the highest-paying job with a communications degree?
A2: Some of the highest paying jobs with a communications degree include public relations manager, corporate communications manager, media planning director, advertising executive, brand strategist, and content strategist.

Q3: What is the scope of BA Communicative English?
A3: A BA in Communicative English offers diverse career opportunities. Students can work in media, journalism, content creation, and public relations. Other fields include corporate communication and language instruction. This degree prepares students for roles in both private and public sectors.

Q4: Can I become a teacher after my BA in Communicative English?
A4: Yes. Students can become teachers after completing a BA in Communicative English. However, they must complete a teacher training programme (like a Bachelor of Education) to be fully certified for teaching positions in most regions.

Q5: What is BA Human Resource Management?
A5: A Bachelor of Arts in Human Resource Management (BA HRM) is an undergraduate degree that teaches students how to manage people in an organisation. Students also learn about organisational communication, employee recruitment and development processes, labour law and relations, and management theories.

Q6: How to get into employee relations?
A6: To enter the field of employee relations, students typically need a bachelor's degree in human resources, business management, or a related field. They also need to gain relevant experience in HR roles with a focus on employee relations and develop strong communication and interpersonal skills.

Q7: What is an example of employee relations?
A7: An example of employee relations would be a situation where a company's HR department mediates a conflict between two employees, helping them resolve a disagreement about work responsibilities.
