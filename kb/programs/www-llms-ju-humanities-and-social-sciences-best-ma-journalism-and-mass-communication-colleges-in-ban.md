Q7: What is MA Mass Communication?
A7: A Master of Arts (MA) in Mass Communication is a two-year postgraduate degree that focuses on the study of how information is communicated to large audiences through various media channels, such as print, television, radio, and digital platforms.

Q8: Who is the father of Mass Communication?
A8: Wilbur Schramm, an American scholar who helped establish the field of communication studies, is known as the "Father of Mass Communication."
