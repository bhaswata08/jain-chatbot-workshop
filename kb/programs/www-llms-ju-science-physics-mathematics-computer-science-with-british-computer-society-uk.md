FAQ's:
Q1. Can I do BSc CS without Maths?
A1: No. Students cannot do a BSc in Computer Science (CS) without passing mathematics in 12th grade. Mathematics is a mandatory subject for admission to BSc CS programmes.

Q2. Does BSc Computer Science have Physics?
A2: Yes. BSc computer science includes physics-based principles and concepts. These principles are used to solve technical problems related to software, hardware, and network architecture.

Q3. Can I join ISRO after BSc Computer Science?
A3: Yes. Students can join ISRO after a B.Sc. in computer science if they meet their eligibility criteria. They should have a minimum of 65% marks or a CGPA of 6.84/10 in the undergraduate degree from a recognised university. Additionally, students need to pass a written test and an interview.

Q4. Is Maths, Physics, and Computer Science a good combination?
A4: Yes. Combining Maths, Physics, and Computer can complement each other well, providing a strong foundation in analytical thinking, problem-solving, and practical application.

Q5. How many years is BSc Physics?
A5: A Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Physics is a three-year undergraduate programme.

Q6. Is a BSc in Computer Science good?
A6: A Bachelor of Science in Computer Science can be a good career choice. Students can work as software engineers or data analysts in an MNC, start their own business as an IT consultant, or work in government firms and academia.

Q7. Who is eligible for BSc Computer Science?
A7: To be eligible for a BSc in Computer Science, students must have passed their 10+2 or equivalent exam in the science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).

Q8. Is BSc Computer Science hard?
A8: Pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science can be challenging, but it can also be rewarding. The difficulty level depends on the student's learning capacity and interest in the subject.

Q9. Is BSc Computer Science 4 years?
A9: A BSc in Computer Science is usually a three-year course divided into six semesters. However, some institutions may offer an extended four-year programme. Students must check the admission guidelines before enrolling in the programme.

Q10. Can I join ISRO after BSc Physics?
A10: Yes. Students can join ISRO after completing a BSc in Physics, but they typically need to pursue further education, such as an MSc in Physics or a related field, to be eligible for most scientific positions within the organisation.

Q11. Is Maths compulsory for BSc Physics?
A11: Yes. Since students will study Quantum Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Optics, Calculus, Semiconductors, Waves, Statistics, and so on, it is important to have a strong understanding of Math to understand the basic concepts in the curriculum.

Q12. Is BSc Physics hard?
A12: A BSc in Physics can be rigorous because it involves advanced theories, numerical problems, and extensive practical work.

Q13. Is BSc Maths good for UPSC?
A13: Generally, subjects like Geography, Economics, Agriculture, Environmental Science, and Biology (Botany/Zoology) can provide valuable knowledge for UPSC exams. However, BSc Maths can help students effectively prepare for quantitative and reasoning questions.

Q14. How to join ISRO after BSc Maths?
A14: To join ISRO after a BSc in Maths, students must consider pursuing a Master's degree in a relevant field like Physics, Astrophysics, or Computational Mathematics. ISRO typically prefers candidates with a postgraduate degree in a science-related field for most scientific positions.

Q15. Is BSc Maths worth it?
A15: Yes. Pursuing a BSc in Mathematics can be worthwhile because it can lead to a variety of well-paid jobs and help students develop analytical and problem-solving skills.

Q16. Which government job is best after BSc Maths?
A16: After a BSc in Mathematics, some of the best government job options include: Bank Probationary Officer (PO), Statistical Investigator, Assistant Section Officer (ASO) in various government departments, Teacher/Lecturer in government schools, Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in research organisations, and positions in the Indian Defence Services.
