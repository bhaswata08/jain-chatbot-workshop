FAQ's:
Q1. How to become a Neuropsychologist in India?
A1: To become a Neuropsychologist in India, students need to pursue undergraduate studies in Neuroscience or Psychology followed by a Master's degree, such as a Postgraduate Diploma in Neuropsychology. Additionally, students need to gain hands-on experience through internships at hospitals or clinics specialising in neurological conditions.

Q2. Is Neuropsychology a doctor?
A2: No. Neuropsychologists are not doctors. Rather than prescribing medication or conducting medical procedures, they focus on administering tests and interpreting the results. A professional with a medical degree working in this field is known as a neuropsychiatrist.

Q3. Does Neuropsychology require NEET?
A3: No. NEET is not required to become a neuropsychologist in India. Instead, students need to complete a bachelor's degree in psychology or neuroscience and then get a Master's degree, such as a Postgraduate Diploma in Neuropsychology. They must also get licensed by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).

Q4. What is the difference between Psychology and Neuropsychology?
A4: Psychology focuses on mental processes and behaviour from a broader perspective. Contrarily, Neuropsychology specifically examines how brain function impacts behaviour, particularly in cases of brain damage or neurological disorders.
