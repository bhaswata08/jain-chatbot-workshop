FAQ's:
Q1. What is Film and Media?
A1: Film and media are forms of communication that use moving images, sound, and other technologies to convey ideas, stories, and messages.

Q2. Is Media a good career?
A2: Yes. Media is a promising career as it offers diverse opportunities in industries, such as television, radio, film, digital media, journalism, publishing, PR, marketing, and gaming. Graduates can explore creative and dynamic roles across various fields.

Q3. What is the concept of Media?
A3: Media" refers to the various channels or tools used to store, transmit, and deliver information to a large audience. It includes platforms like newspapers, radio, television, the internet, social media, and magazines to inform, entertain, and influence people on a wide scale.

Q4. How to study Media?
A4: To study Media, students can pursue an undergraduate degree such as a Bachelor of Arts (B.A) in Film and Media. Students will study media theories, including how media shapes public opinion, influences social norms, and constructs narratives in this course.

Q5. How to get a job in Media?
A5: To get a job in Media, students must gain work experience by contacting radio stations, TV channels, newspapers, PR, or advertising agencies for opportunities. Participating in university media activities like radio or publications also adds value. Some courses offer work placements, which help develop professional skills and industry connections.
