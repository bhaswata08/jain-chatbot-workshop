FAQ's:
Q1. What is the scope of communication?
A1: The scope of communication is wide and pervasive, covering many aspects of life, including personal, social, business, and cultural. It is used to express ideas, emotions, and objectives, and helps build relationships with others.

Q2. What comes under Communication Design?
A2: Communication Design encompasses a range of visual design practices used to convey a message, including branding, advertising, graphic design, user interface (UI) design, web design, etc.

Q3. Is Visual Communication Design a good career?
A3: Yes. Visual Communication can be considered a good career choice due to its growing demand in the digital landscape and diverse job opportunities across industries such as advertising, branding, and e-Commerce.

Q4. What is BDes in Communication Design?
A4: A Bachelor of Design (BDes) in Communication Design is an undergraduate degree that teaches students how to convey messages using Visual Communication and Graphic Design.

Q5. What does a Communication Designer do?
A5: A communication designer creates visual content to convey a message to a target audience. They use their skills in design, marketing, and copywriting to create engaging and effective campaigns.
