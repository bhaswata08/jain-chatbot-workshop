FAQ's:

Q1. Is Biotechnology a good career?
A1: Yes. Biotechnology is a good career choice. It offers exciting opportunities for those interested in Biology, Genetics, and Scientific applications.

Q2. What is MSc Biotechnology?
A2: A Master of Science in Biotechnology (MSc Biotechnology) is a two-year postgraduate programme that teaches students how to apply Biological Sciences to create new products and technologies.

Q3. Which entrance exam is required for MSc Biotechnology?
A3: There are multiple entrance exams for admission to an MSc Biotechnology programme, including the Common University Entrance Test - Postgraduate (CUET-PG), Indian Institute of Technology Joint Admission Test for Masters (IIT JAM), Andhra Pradesh Post Graduate Common Entrance Test (AP PGCET), and Common Post Graduate Entrance Tests (CPGET). Some colleges also conduct university-level exams.

Q4. Is Biotechnology in demand?
A4: Yes. Biotechnology is a growing field with many opportunities in Healthcare, Agriculture, Environmental science, and Industrial biotechnology.

Q5. Can a Biotechnologist work in ISRO?
A5: A Biotechnologist cannot directly join ISRO as the eligibility criteria require a BE/BTech degree with a minimum of 65% marks or a CGPA of 6.84. However, if they pursue engineering disciplines relevant to ISRO, they may qualify through exams like JEE Advanced, KVPY, or IISER Aptitude Test.

Q6. Who is eligible for MSc Biotechnology?
A6: To be eligible for an MSc in Biotechnology, candidates must have a BSc in Biotechnology or a related field such as Botany, Zoology, Microbiology, or Biochemistry. The degree must be from a recognised university, with at least 50% aggregate marks. Some colleges offer merit-based admission, while others require candidates to clear entrance exams like CUET-PG, ICAR AIEEA, IIT JAM, AP PGCET, and CPGET.

Q7. Can a Biotechnologist work in a hospital?
A7: Yes. Biotechnologists can work in hospitals. They work in hospital laboratories, and may also work in other settings like universities, factories, and government labs.
