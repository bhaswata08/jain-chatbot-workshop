FAQ's:
Q1: Who is the father of Sociology?
A1: Auguste Comte is recognised as the father of sociology. A French philosopher, he introduced the term "sociology" in the 1830s and laid the foundation for the field.

Q2: Is Sociology easy?
A2: Sociology can be challenging and requires critical thinking, so it's not necessarily easy. However, it can be relatively easy to understand for people interested in studying human behaviour and society.

Q3: Is Sociology in demand?
A3: Yes. Sociology is in demand, with a growing job market for sociologists, particularly in fields like social research, policy analysis, market research, social work, and community organising. Employers across various sectors highly value the skills developed in Sociology.

Q4: What is Sociology BA 1st year?
A4: In Sociology's first year, students explore fundamental concepts related to society, social interactions, and social structures. They learn how individuals are influenced by their social environment. The curriculum often includes introductory topics such as sociological theories, research methods, and social stratification.

Q5: Is BA Sociology good for UPSC?
A5: Yes. BA Sociology is ideal for UPSC because it has a lesser scope compared to other subjects, which makes it easier to learn in a shorter duration. Plus, it allows good grades because questions are usually basic.
There is some overlap with General Studies Paper 1, which assists with the overall preparation. Also, sociology is very important for civil services as it offers knowledge about society, social structures, institutions, policies, and their significance in governance and policy-making.

Q6: Can I do PhD in Sociology?
A6: Yes. Students can pursue a PhD in Sociology. A PhD in Sociology can prepare students for careers in research and teaching.

Q7: Which job is best for Sociology?
A7: Sociology offers varied careers in fields such as criminology, social work, community service, journalism, and others. Students can become policy analysts, human resources executives, survey researchers, volunteers, etc.

Q8: Does Sociology have scope?
A8: Yes. Sociology has a wide scope and can lead to many different careers. Sociology is a social science that studies how societies work, including how social institutions like family, education, and government affect people and society.

Q9: What is the scope of a BA in Psychology?
A9: A Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Psychology opens up various career paths in counselling, mental health, and human resources. Students can also pursue higher education by opting for master's degrees and a Doctorate of Philosophy (PhD).

Q10: Is BA Psychology a good career?
A10: Yes. A BA in Psychology is a good career choice for individuals interested in pursuing opportunities in fields like healthcare, education, business, and social services. Students can also advance their careers by opting for postgraduate studies in their areas of interest.

Q11: Is there any entrance exam for Psychology?
A11: Yes. There are entrance exams for psychology courses. These include the Common University Entrance Test (CUET), Jamia Millia Islamia Entrance Exam (JMI Entrance Exam), Council of Scientific and Industrial Research National Eligibility Test (CSIR NET), and University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test (UGC NET).
In addition, some universities and states conduct their own entrance exams, such as the JAIN Entrance Test-Undergraduate (JET-UG) for psychology programmes.

Q12: Does Psychology need NEET?
A12: No. Students don't need to apply for NEET to pursue a psychology degree. NEET is mainly used for admission to medical and dental undergraduate programmes.

Q13: Is Psychology hard to study?
A13: Yes. Psychology can be challenging to study as it requires significant time and dedication. The four-year bachelor's programme develops critical thinking and analytical skills while covering a broad range of topics rather than focusing on a single subject.
The workload can be demanding, but the degree provides valuable knowledge applicable to various career fields such as counselling, marketing, and working with people.

Q14: Can I do PhD after my BA in Psychology?
A14: Yes. Students can do PhD after a BA in Psychology. They apply for the doctorate by selecting subjects per the NET examination and appearing for the chosen topic.

Q15: What is the salary for a BA in Psychology?
A15: Students can earn between INR 3 - 7 LPA after graduation. With higher education and more experience, they can earn up to INR 24 LPA.

Q16: What is the highest-paid job in Psychology?
A16: Neuropsychologists and psychiatrists are among the highest-paid jobs in psychology.
