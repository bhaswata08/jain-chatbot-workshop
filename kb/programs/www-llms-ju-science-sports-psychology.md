FAQ's:
Q1. Who is the father of Sports Psychology?
A1: Dr. Carl Diem is referred to as the father of Sports Psychology.

Q2. How to become a Sports Psychologist in India?
A2: To become a sports psychologist in India, students must complete a Bachelor's degree in Psychology, followed by a Master's degree such as a Postgraduate Diploma in Sports Psychology. Moreover, students can also gain experience through internships and fieldwork.

Q3. What is the scope of Sports Psychology?
A3: Sports Psychology is a broad field that helps athletes and other people improve their performance and experience in sports. It also helps people manage their health and transition after their athletic careers.
