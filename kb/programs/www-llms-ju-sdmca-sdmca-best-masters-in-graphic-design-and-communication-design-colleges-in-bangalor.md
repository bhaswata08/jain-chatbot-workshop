FAQ's:
Q1. Is Graphic Design a good career?
A1: Graphic design can be a good career because of the growing need for visual communication in industries such as advertising, marketing, and media. Students can access different roles in graphic design, including brand and logo design, advertising design, and user experience (UX) design.

Q2. Is Communication Design a good career?
A2: Yes. Communication Design can be a good career choice if students enjoy being creative and can use their artistic vision to solve problems and communicate ideas effectively. It is also ideal for students with strong visual communication skills to influence audiences through design.

Q3. What is Design in Graphic Design?
A3: Designing in Graphic Design is the process of creating visual content to communicate ideas and messages. It involves combining visual elements like images, colours, and typography to create a layout that evokes specific emotions.

Q4. What do communication designers do?
A4: A communication designer creates visual content to convey a message to a target audience. They are responsible for creating designs that align with a brand's guidelines.

Q5. What is Design and Communication Graphics?
A5: Design and Communication Graphics is a field of study focusing on using visual elements like drawings, diagrams, and 3D models to communicate complex information effectively.

Q6. What is Communication Design?
A6: Communication design is a discipline that focuses on creating visual elements like images, typography, and layouts to convey a message to a specific audience effectively.

Q7. Is Graphic Design hard?
A7: While learning the basic graphic designing tools isn't necessarily "hard," mastering the craft and becoming a successful graphic designer can be challenging. Developing a strong design aesthetic, understanding the nuances of colour, and managing client deadlines require consistent practice and efficient time management.

Q8. Why do we study Graphic Communication?
A8: We study Graphic Communication to learn how to convey information visually through design elements like images, typography, and layout. It helps us to communicate complex ideas clearly and engagingly across various media platforms.
