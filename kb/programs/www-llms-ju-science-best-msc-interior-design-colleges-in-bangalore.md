FAQ's:

Q1. What is MSc Interior Design?
A1. An MSc in Interior Design is a postgraduate degree that teaches students how to design and plan interior spaces. It's a research-based program covering design theory, spatial planning, and sustainability.

Q2. Is Interior Design in high demand?
A2. Yes. Interior Design is in high demand, especially in urban areas where real estate is a growing industry. People are investing in making their homes and businesses more functional and beautiful, so there is a steady demand in this field.

Q3. Can you get a PhD in Interior Design?
A3. Yes. Students can get a PhD in Interior Design. Typically, they need a master's degree in Design, Architecture, MTech, or M.E. Alternatively, those with a four-year bachelor's degree (with at least 75% marks) and three years of relevant experience in teaching, research, or industry may also qualify for the doctoral programme.

Q4. Is Interior Design a high paying job?
A4. Yes. Interior Design can be a high-paying job, especially for individuals specialising in high-end residential or commercial projects.
