Q1. Is Counselling Psychology a good career?
A1: The demand for mental health professionals is increasing across India. Psychologists and counsellors play a key role in addressing various mental health concerns. Their expertise is highly valued in corporate offices, mental health facilities, and educational institutions.

Q2. What is the difference between Clinical and Counselling Psychology?
A2: Clinical psychologists focus on mental disorders while counselling psychologists focus on personal and social well-being.

Q3. How do I become a certified Counsellor?
A3: To become a certified counsellor, students need to earn a Master's degree in Counselling or a related field like Psychology or Social work. This also includes supervised practicum and internship hours, followed by obtaining a professional certification from a recognised body.

Q4. Can I become a Counsellor without a Psychology degree?
A4: It is possible to build a career in Counselling without a degree by exploring different fields. Options include peer counselling, life skills counselling, and drug and alcohol counselling. Getting a relevant certification is essential to gain expertise and credibility in these areas.
