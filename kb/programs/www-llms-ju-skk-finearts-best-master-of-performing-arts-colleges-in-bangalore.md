FAQ's:
Q1: What is a Master of Performing Arts?
A1: Master of Performing Arts is a popular postgraduate programme that provides in-depth study and training in various streams of Arts. These streams include music, dance, theatre, etc.

Q2: Is singing a performing art?
A2: Yes. Singing is a type of performing art. Singing involves a combination of several skills like vocal expression and knowledge of keys, rhythm, breath control, etc.

Q3: What comes under performing arts?
A3: Performing arts include music, dance, drama, theatre, mime, puppetry, and other live performance forms. These art forms involve creativity, expression, and audience interaction.
