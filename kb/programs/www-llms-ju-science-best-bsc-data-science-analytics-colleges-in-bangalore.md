FAQ's:
Q1. Is Data Scientist a stressful job?
A1: Data Science can be stressful as data scientists often have to work with large amounts of data, complex problems, and tight deadlines. They also need to keep up with evolving technologies. However, if one can manage time properly and prioritise their tasks, it can be handled well.

Q2. What are the subjects in Data Science?
A2: Data science courses cover a variety of subjects, including Statistics, Programming, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

Q3. Who is eligible for Data Analyst?
A3: To be eligible for a Data Analytics programme, students must enrol in a Bachelor's degree (Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Data Science and Analytics. Additionally, students can specialise in the field by opting for a Master of Business Administration (MBA) or Postgraduate Diploma in Data Science and Analytics.

Q4. Is Data Science Coding?
A4: Yes. Data science heavily involves coding, a significant instrument in the employment of, study, synthesis, and visualisation of data to provide useful information.

Q5. Is Data Science Math heavy?
A5: Yes. Data Science is built on Math, including calculus, linear algebra, and statistics. Data scientists use Math to analyse data, build models, and discover insights. Therefore, students need to gain proficient knowledge in subjects such as Mathematics, Statistics, And Programming.

Q6. Can I do Data Analyst after the 12th?
A6: Yes. Students can become data analysts after Class 12th by opting for a Bachelor's programme in the relevant field, such as a BSc in Data Science and Analytics.

Q7. Is Data Science difficult to study?
A7: Yes. Data science can be challenging to study, but it's also a rewarding field with a high demand for skilled professionals.

Q8. Is Data Science a high-paying job?
A8: Yes. Data Science offers high-paying jobs in different sectors, especially in top tech and analytics firms. Some of the most high-paying job roles include data scientists, machine learning engineers, and data analysts.

Q9. Who is eligible for BSc Data Science?
A9: To be eligible for a BSc in Data Science programme, students need to score a minimum of 55% in 10+2 from a recognised board in the Science stream. Students must have Mathematics and Statistics as core subjects in their 10+2.

Q10. Can I do a BSc in Data Science without Maths?
A10: No. Students cannot pursue a BSc in Data Science without some Math. Statistics, calculus, and linear algebra are the most relevant areas of the Math for Data Science syllabus.

Q11. What is the salary of BSc Data Science?
A11: The salary for a data scientist in India can range from INR 4 - 57 LPA, depending on experience and job role

Q12. Is Data Science a good career?
A12: With the help of Data Science, it is possible to turn a business problem into a research project, which can then be converted back into an actual solution. Due to the existence of so many Data Science positions and the lucrative compensation, a Data Science career is one of the most sought-after ones.

Q13. Is Data Science in demand?
A13: Yes. Data Science is in high demand, and various industries have a strong job outlook. The field is growing rapidly as companies use data to improve quality and financial growth

Q14. Is Data Science hard?
A14: Data Science can be challenging, requiring proficiency in various technologies and tools, such as SQL, Python, R, and machine learning libraries. But it's not impossible to learn. With the proper preparation and determination, students can thrive and build a strong foundation for a successful career.
