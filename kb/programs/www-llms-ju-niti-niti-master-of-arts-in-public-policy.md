FAQ's:
Q1: What is an MA in Public Policy?
A1: MA in Public Policy is a postgraduate degree that focuses on policy analysis, governance, and decision-making in public affairs.

Q2: Who is the father of public policy?
A2: Woodrow Wilson is often considered the 'Father of Public Policy' for his contributions to Public Administration and governance.

Q3: How to become a public policy analyst?
A3: To become a public policy analyst, students can earn a degree in Public Policy or a related field, gain analytical skills, and gain experience through internships or research roles.

Q4: How to start a career in Public Policy?
A4: Enrol in MA in Public Policy. Gain experience in government or NGOs, network with professionals, and stay updated on policy trends. All these actions will help start a career in Public Policy.

Q5: What is the difference between Public Administration and Public policy?
A5: Public Policy focuses on making laws and regulations, while Public Administration deals with implementing and managing them.

Q6: What is the nature of Public Policy?
A6: Public Policy is a versatile subject. It focuses on the study of policies that contribute to the welfare of the public. Public Policy also develops an understanding of societal issues. Public Policy has a great role to play in government action. Dynamic public policies shape laws, address social and economic issues, and improve governance for the welfare of society.
