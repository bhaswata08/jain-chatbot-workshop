FAQ's:
Q1. What is BSc in Interior Design?
A1: A Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Interior Design is an undergraduate programme that teaches students how to design spaces. It's an interdisciplinary programme that combines design, engineering, architecture, and fine arts.

Q2. What is the highest paid Interior Design job?
A2: Some of the highest-paid Interior Design jobs include corporate, healthcare, and government interior designing. Other high-paying jobs include project manager, landscape architect, and museum exhibit designer.

Q3. Do Interior Designers need Math?
A3: Yes. Interior Designers need basic Math skills for their daily work. They must calculate room dimensions, fabric measurements, and other essential design elements.

Q4. Which exam is required for Interior Designing?
A4: There are several exams for admission to Interior Design programmes, such as:
. Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design (UCEED)
. National Institute of Design Entrance Exam (NID)
. National Institute of Fashion Technology Entrance Exam (NIFT)
. Common University Entrance Test (CUET)

Q5. Does Interior Design have a future?
A5: Yes. The future of Interior Design is bright. The field owes its dynamism to the rise in the application of personalised spaces and application of new technologies. On top of that, the increase in the real estate industry means more building projects and therefore a higher demand for interior design services.

Q6. Is Interior Design a stressful job?
A6: Every job can be stressful due to several reasons. A career in Interior design can also be challenging due to tight deadlines, managing client expectations, and balancing creative vision with practical needs. However, effective communication, clear project planning, and setting realistic expectations can help manage stress.

Q7. Can I do Interior Designing after 12th Arts?
A7: Yes. Students can pursue interior design after 12th in the Arts stream. Interior design is a creative field that values passion over specific academic backgrounds.
