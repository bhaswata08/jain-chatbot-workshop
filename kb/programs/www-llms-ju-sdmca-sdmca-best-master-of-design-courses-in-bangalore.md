FAQ's:
Q1. What is Human Centered Design?
A1: Human-Centred Design (HCD) is a design approach that focuses on the needs and experiences of people. It's a way of solving problems by putting people at the centre of the design process.
Q2. Why Human Centered Design?
A2: Human-Centered Design is important because it prioritises understanding and actively involving the needs, wants, and perspectives of the end-user throughout the design process.
Q3. What are the 4 principles of Human Centered Design?
A3: The four principles of Human-Centered design (HCD) are:
● Empathise: Understand the needs and perspectives of the users
● Define: Synthesise the information gathered about the users
● Understand the problem: Understand the problem, the people, and the system
● Iterate: Continually prototype, test, and refine products and services
