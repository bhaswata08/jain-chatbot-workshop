FAQ's:
Q1. Is Media Studies a good degree?
A1: Yes. A Media Studies degree can be a good choice as it provides a comprehensive understanding of the media landscape, critical analysis skills, and practical knowledge in various media production areas.

Q2. Is a Master's in Film worth it?
A2: Pursuing a Master's in Film can be helpful for individuals who want to gain a deeper understanding of the film industry, develop advanced technical skills, and build a strong portfolio. It is recommended if one aspires to take on a more specialised or leadership role within filmmaking.

Q3. What is a Film degree called?
A3: A degree in film can be called a Bachelor of Arts (BA), Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA), Master of Arts (MA) in Film and Media or Doctor of Philosophy (PhD). The specific degree students wish to pursue depends on the type of programme, their career goals and their current educational qualification.

Q4. Is a Film degree useful?
A4: Yes. A Film degree can be useful for students as it can open doors to various careers within production, editing, directing, cinematography, and even related fields like marketing and media production.

Q5. Can you get a PhD in Film?
A5: Yes. Students can pursue a PhD in Film that focuses on the academic analysis and research of cinema.

Q6. Is Filmmaking a good career?
A6: Filmmaking is a highly desired career choice for creative individuals from all backgrounds. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labour Statistics, employment for film and video professionals is expected to grow by 29% by 2030.
