FAQ's:
Q1. Is coding required for Data Science?
A1: Yes. Coding is an essential skill for data science. It helps data scientists analyse data, create models, and communicate findings.

Q2. Is MSc Data Science hard?
A2: A Master's in Data Science can be challenging since students need a solid background in Math, Statistics, and Computer Programming. The curriculum also involves learning new technologies and tools like SQL, Python, R, and machine learning libraries. However, it can be rewarding with effective time management and a well-planned study routine.

Q3. Who is eligible for MSc Data Science?
A3: To be eligible for an MSc in Data Science, students typically need a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science/ Mathematics/ Statistics or any related and relevant field with a minimum aggregate of 60% from a recognised university.

Q4. What is the scope of Data Analytics?
A4: Data analytics has a wide scope, including business, healthcare, finance, marketing, and more. It can help businesses make better decisions by analysing data.

Q5. What is Data Science and Analytics all about?
A5: Data Science and Analytics is a domain that covers activities such as collecting, cleansing, and analysis of information using statistical methods, machine learning, and other computational techniques. It draws out valuable information and gives well-timed predictions about upcoming trends.
