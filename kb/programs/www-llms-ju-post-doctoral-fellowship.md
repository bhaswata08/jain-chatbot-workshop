Q1. Do postdoctoral fellows get paid?
A1: Yes. Postdoctoral fellows receive a stipend or salary for their research work. The pay varies based on the type of fellowship or grant they hold. Independently funded fellowships often offer higher salaries, but postdoc income is taxable and usually not adjusted for inflation.

Q2. What comes after a postdoc?
A2: After a postdoc, students can pursue a variety of career paths, including academic, research, and other professional roles. They can become research scientists, grant managers within a funding body, data analysts, medical/scientific writers, or project managers in any field.

Q3. What is the salary of a postdoctoral fellow?
A3: The salary for a postdoctoral fellowship in India can range from ₹30,000 to ₹71,769 per month, depending on the fellowship, grant, and institution.

Q4. Can you do a postdoc without a PhD?
A4: No. By definition, one cannot do a postdoc without a PhD. A "Postdoc" stands for "postdoctoral" and is a research position that is designed specifically for people who have already finished their doctoral degree.

Q5. What is the difference between a PhD and a postdoctoral?
A5: A PhD is a doctoral degree earned after completing advanced research in a specific field. Postdoctoral is a temporary research role taken after a PhD to gain more specialised experience. It helps researchers develop their careers before entering permanent academic or industry roles.
