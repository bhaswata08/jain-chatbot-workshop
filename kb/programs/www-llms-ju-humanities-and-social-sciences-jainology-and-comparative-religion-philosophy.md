FAQ's:
Q1: What are the 5 main points of Jainism?
A1: The five main points of Jainism are non-violence, truth, non-stealing, non-attachment, and continence. These are also known as the five vows of Jainism.

Q2: Who is the Guru of Jainism?
A2: In Jainism, a Guru or Tirthankara is a saviour and the highest preacher of dharma (the righteous path). The first Tirthankara was Rishabhdev, while the last was Mahavira. According to Jain scriptures, the philosophy of Jainism was formalised by the last Tirthankara, Vardhaman Mahavir.

Q3: Who is the father of Philosophy?
A3: Socrates, a Greek philosopher, is known as the father of Western philosophy.

Q4: Is Philosophy a good career?
A4: Yes. Philosophy can be a promising career as it offers diverse job opportunities across various sectors. Graduates can work in publishing, media, journalism, advertising, teaching, computing and IT.

Q5: What is the scope of Philosophy?
A5: The scope of philosophy is broad and includes the study of reality, knowledge, and values. It also influences many aspects of human life, such as politics, society, and economics.

Q6: Is studying Philosophy hard?
A6: Philosophy can be challenging, but it can also be rewarding. It requires critical thinking, analytical skills, and the ability to explain concepts clearly.

Q7: How many years is MA Philosophy?
A7: A Master of Arts in Philosophy (MA Philosophy) is a two-year course.
