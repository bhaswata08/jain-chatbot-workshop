FAQ's:
Q1. Is NEET required for Forensic Science?
A1: No. NEET is not required for admission to undergraduate Forensic Science programmes in India. It is generally needed to get admission to dental or medical courses in India.

Q2. Is there any entrance exam for Forensic Science?
A2: Yes. There are entrance exams for Forensic Science, including the All India Forensic Science Entrance Test (AIFSET), Common University Entrance Test (CUET), University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test (UGC NET), and other college-specific entrance exams.

Q3. Is Forensic a government job?
A3: Yes. One can access government jobs after graduating in Forensic Science. They can work in law enforcement, such as the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), and crime investigation agencies.

Q4. Is Forensics a high-paying job?
A4: Yes. Forensic experts can earn well if they possess the desired skill set, knowledge and experience. Forensic medical examiners are among the highest paid forensic science careers.

Q5. What is the eligibility for BSc Forensic Science?
A5: To be eligible for a BSc in Forensic Science, students must pass Class 12 with at least 50% aggregate marks in the Science stream from a recognised board. They must also have Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology (PCM/B) as core subjects in their 10+2.

Q6. What is the salary of BSc Forensic Science?
A6: The average salary of a forensic expert in India is INR 3.5 LPA. They can earn up to 20 LPAs based on their skill and expertise.
