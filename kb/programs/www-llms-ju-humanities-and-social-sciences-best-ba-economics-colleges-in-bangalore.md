FAQ's:
Q1: Is a BA in Economics a good degree?
A1: A Bachelor of Arts in Economics can be a good degree if students are interested in economics and the development of societies. It can lead to various careers in business, government, finance, banking, healthcare, consulting and other sectors.

Q2: What is demand in Economics?
A2: In economics, demand is the desire and ability of consumers to buy goods and services at a given price. It's a fundamental economic concept that helps determine prices and availability.

Q3: Is Economics a hard course?
A3: The study of economics can be tough at the undergraduate level because coursework integrates business, mathematics, accounting, sociology, and psychology. Nonetheless, it remains a worthwhile career option due to its scope. However, with adequate preparation, students may sail seamlessly through the programme due to the abundance of varied opportunities available in the field.

Q4: Is Economics a lot of Math?
A4: While not purely Math, Economics does involve a significant amount of mathematics, especially at higher levels of study. Concepts like calculus, statistics, and linear algebra are used to build and analyse economic models.

Q5: What is income demand in Economics?
A5: In economics, income demand is the quantity of a product or service a consumer is willing to buy at a given income level. It's a measure of how responsive demand is to changes in income.

Q6: Is Maths compulsory for BA Economics?
A6: No. Maths is not compulsory at 10+2 to pursue a BA in Economics.

Q7: What is the highest-paid job in Economics?
A7: Some of the highest-paying jobs in Economics include economist, market research analyst, financial analyst, and business analyst, among others.

Q8: Is BA Economics good for an MBA?
A8: Yes. A BA in Economics is considered a good foundation for pursuing an MBA, as the analytical skills and understanding of economic principles gained in an economics degree can significantly complement the business knowledge acquired in an MBA programme.

Q9: What are the four types of demand in Economics?
A9: The four main types of demand in economics are price demand, income demand, cross demand, and composite demand.
