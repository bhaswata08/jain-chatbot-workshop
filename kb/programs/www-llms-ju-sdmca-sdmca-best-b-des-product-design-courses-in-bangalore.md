FAQ's:
Q1. What do you mean by Product Design?
A1: Product Design is creating a physical or digital product that fulfils the user's need and solves their problem. It's a key part of developing new products for businesses.

Q2. Why study Product Design?
A2: Studying Product Design can help students develop creativity and design skills and access a range of career options in today's job market. Product design can lead to opportunities in design research, desktop publishing, art direction, and more.

Q3. Is Product Design a tech job?
A3: Yes. Product Design is considered a tech job, as it primarily involves creating digital products like software applications and websites within the tech industry. It requires technical skills such as user experience (UX) design, user interface (UI) design, wireframing, prototyping, and proficiency in design software.

Q4. Is Product Design a good career?
A4: Yes. Product design can be a promising career option for students with creative and analytical skills that can help them work across different industries. They can encash the growing demand for skilled designers to draw competitive salaries by creating functional and aesthetically pleasing products.

Q5. Does Product Design require Math?
A5: Yes. Product Design requires Math to some extent. It helps designers and product managers approach problems logically, break down complex issues into smaller parts, and enhance creative problem-solving skills.

Q6. Does Product Design require coding?
A6: While it may be helpful, Product Design doesn't require students to learn coding.

Q7. How do you become a product designer after the 12th?
A7: After 12th, to become a product designer, one should do a B.Des in Product Design from a recognised institution. It will equip one with the necessary skills to enter the field. These include comprehending user needs and doing prototyping and design per usable principles.
