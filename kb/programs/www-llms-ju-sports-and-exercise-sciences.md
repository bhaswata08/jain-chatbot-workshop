Program Description:
Jain University's B.Sc. in Sports and Exercise Sciences is designed to equip students with skills for leadership roles in health and human performance. The interdisciplinary curriculum, supported by departments like Nutrition, Psychology, and Physiotherapy, covers human movement, sports physiology, and performance analysis, preparing students for careers in sports and fitness. Key Subjects: Exercise Physiology, Sports Psychology, Biomechanics, Sports Nutrition, Performance Analysis.

Career Opportunities: Sports Scientist, Performance Analyst, Fitness Consultant, Sports Coach, Rehabilitation Specialist.

FAQ's:
Q1. How to become a Sports Scientist?
A1: Students can begin by enrolling in a BSc in Sports Science course and later specialise in the field by pursuing a Master's degree. Several universities and colleges in India and abroad offer specialised programmes in Sports Science.

Q2. What is the highest paying Sports Science job?
A2: Some of the highest-paying Sports Science jobs include sports medicine physician, sports psychologist, sports scientist, and sports management executive.

Q3. How to study Sports Science?
A3: To study sports science, students complete their 10+2 education with at least 50% marks in any stream-Science, Commerce, or Arts-while English is compulsory. A strong foundation in the Sciences, particularly Human Biology and Psychology, is vital. Additionally, having a keen interest in sports is essential for success in this field.
