FAQ's:
Q1. Does Graphic Design have a future?
A1: Yes. Graphic Design has a promising future. With the rise of technology such as artificial intelligence and changing consumer preferences, graphic designers will be important in creating visual experiences.

Q2. Is Graphic Design a good career?
A2: Yes. Since there's a growing need for visual communication in industries such as advertising, marketing, and media, students can pursue different roles in Graphic Design.

Q3. Which type of Graphic Design is best?
A3: Some of the most popular types of graphic designing include web design, UI and interactive design, advertising and marketing design, motion graphics and animation, packaging design, and game development.

Q4. Is a BA in Communication a good degree?
A4: Yes. A BA in Communication can be considered a good degree as it equips students with valuable transferable skills like critical thinking, writing, verbal communication, and strategic planning. These skills make them employable across various industries, such as marketing, public relations, journalism, and business development.

Q5. Does Graphic Design require coding?
A5: No. Coding is not required for Graphic Design; however, basic knowledge of coding can help designers communicate with developers and improve their designs.

Q6. Is Graphic Design in demand?
A6: Yes. Graphic Design is in demand, driven by digital marketing, social media, and e-commerce.

Q7. Who is the father of Graphic Design?
A7: Paul Rand is known as the father of modern Graphic Design.
