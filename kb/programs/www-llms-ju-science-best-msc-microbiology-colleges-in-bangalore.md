FAQ's:
Q1. What is MSc in Microbiology?
A1: An MSc in Microbiology is a postgraduate degree that focuses on the study of microorganisms. It's a two-year programme that includes theoretical and practical components.

Q2. Is MSc Microbiology tough?
A2: An MSc in Microbiology is considered a difficult course compared to other postgraduate programmes. It's a complex field that involves critical thinking, problem-solving, and data analysis.

Q3. Can I do a PhD after MSc Microbiology?
A3: Yes. Students can pursue a PhD after completing an MSc in Microbiology; a Master's degree is the standard prerequisite for admission into a PhD programme in most universities, including for Microbiology.

Q4. Does MSc Microbiology have scope?
A4: Yes. An MSc in Microbiology has a wide scope and can lead to many different careers in Healthcare, Biotechnology, Environmental research, Food production, Pharmaceuticals, Bioremediation, Agriculture, and Food and beverage industries.
