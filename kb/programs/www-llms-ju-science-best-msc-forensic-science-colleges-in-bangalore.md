FAQ's:
Q1. Is Forensic Science a good career?
A1: Yes. Forensic Science can be a rewarding career choice for learners interested in applying scientific principles to legal investigations. This discipline is a relatively new and interdisciplinary field in science and law, and therefore, it holds the potential for meaningful work within the criminal justice system.

Q2. Do we need NEET for Forensic Science?
A2: No. Since Forensic Science is not a medical or dental field, students need not appear for NEET to enrol in a Forensic Science undergraduate programme.

Q3. How to apply for MSc Forensic Science?
A3: To apply for an MSc in Forensic Science, students need to graduate with a Bachelor's degree in Science/Engineering/Pharmacy/ Medicine from a recognised university and at least 60% marks.

Q4. Is MSc Forensic Science worth it?
A4: Yes. An MSc in Forensic Science can be a good choice if students are interested in a career in law and justice. The field is growing, and there are many career opportunities in government and private sectors.

Q5. What is the salary of a Forensic Doctor?
A5: A forensic doctor, also known as a forensic pathologist, typically earns a salary ranging from ₹3 lakhs to ₹10 lakhs per annum in India, depending on their experience, specialisation, and location.

Q6. Are Forensic Scientists called Doctors?
A6: No. Forensic Scientists are not called doctors.  Forensic Pathologists with an MD degree or a Forensic Physician with an MBB followed by an MS/MD degree can be qualified as doctors.
