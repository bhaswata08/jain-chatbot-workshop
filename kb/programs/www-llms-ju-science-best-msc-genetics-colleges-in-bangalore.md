FAQ's:
Q1. Are Geneticists doctors?
A1: Yes. A geneticist is a doctor who studies the connection between genes and health. They diagnose and manage genetic disorders across all age groups from pregnancy onward. Clinical geneticists also engage in research, teaching, and administrative roles to advance genetic studies.

Q2. Can you get a PhD in Genetics?
A2: Yes. Students can pursue a PhD in Genetics. Many universities offer doctoral programmes specifically focused on the study of Genetics. A PhD in Genetics can lead to careers in Academic research, Medical research, Biotechnology companies, Government agencies, or Teaching positions at the university level.

Q3. How do I become a Geneticist?
A3: To become a geneticist, you need a Bachelor's degree in Biology, Genetics, or a related field. For advanced research roles, a Master's degree and possibly a PhD in Genetics or a relevant area are required.
