FAQ's:
Q1. Is Fashion Designing a good career?
A1: Yes. Fashion Designing can be a good career choice if students have a strong passion for creativity and an interest in fashion trends. The industry offers a wide range of career paths, such as fashion designer, textile designer, stylist, fashion buyer, fashion consultant, and more.

Q2. Is Fashion Designing difficult?
A2: Fashion Designing can be considered a difficult field due to its demanding nature, making it one of the highly competitive industries to work in. It requires a blend of creativity, technical skills, business acumen, and the ability to constantly adapt to changing trends.

Q3. What's the highest paying job in Fashion?
A3: In the fashion industry, brand management, creative direction, and retail buying are some of the highest-paying jobs.

Q4. How can I join Fashion Design after the 12th?
A4: To join Fashion Design after 12th, students can enrol in a Bachelor's degree, such as a Bachelor of Arts in Fashion Design from a reputed college.

Q5. What is BA Fashion Design?
A5: A Bachelor of Fashion Design is an undergraduate degree that teaches students how to create original designs for clothing, shoes, and accessories.
