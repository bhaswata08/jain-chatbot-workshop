FAQ's:
Q1. Does Digital Forensics require coding?
A1: Yes. Digital Forensics requires at least a basic understanding of various coding languages. In addition, proficiency in software development proves to be a critical skill that Digital Forensics students should have because of it, they will be able to write scripts to automate tasks, modify data, and create tools.

Q2. Is Digital Forensics in demand?
A2: The high demand for Digital Forensics stems from dependency on digital technology. The emergence of cybercrime has gone one step further as a result of which millions of skilled people are required to this field. Law enforcement, government agencies, and private companies highly seek Digital Forensics investigators.

Q3. What is meant by Digital Forensics?
A3: Digital Forensics is the process of analysing electronic data to identify, acquire, and report on evidence. It's a branch of forensic science often used in criminal investigations.

Q4. Who uses Digital Forensics?
A4: Digital Forensics is used by law enforcement, corporate investigators, national security agencies, cybersecurity teams, human resources professionals, and auditors.

Q5. Is Digital Forensics a good career?
A5: Yes. Digital Forensics is a great career choice due to its high demand and job opportunities. It offers competitive salaries and intellectually stimulating work, especially for those who enjoy solving complex technical puzzles. This field is ideal for individuals with an interest in cybersecurity and investigations.
