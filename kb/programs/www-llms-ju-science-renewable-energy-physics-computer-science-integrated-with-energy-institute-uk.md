FAQ's:
Q1. Is renewable energy the future?
A1: Yes. Renewable energy is expected to be a major part of the future energy mix, and is considered a key way to address climate change.

Q2. What are the 7 types of renewable energy?
A2: The seven types of renewable energy are solar, wind, hydroelectric, geothermal, biomass, tidal, and hydrogen.

Q3. What are 5 advantages of renewable energy?
A3: The five key advantages of renewable energy are listed below:
. Renewable energy sources don't produce greenhouse gas emissions from fossil fuels, and can therefore reduce air pollution and global warming.
. Renewable energy sources, like the sun, will not run out.
. They are not affected by political conflicts or market instability.
. The renewable energy sector generates employment opportunities.
. Cleaner air and reduced pollution lead to healthier lives.

Q4. What is called renewable energy?
A4: Renewable energy is energy that comes from natural sources that are replenished faster than they are used. These sources include wind, solar, water, geothermal, and biomass.

Q5. Is BSc CS a good career option?
A5: Yes. A BSc in Computer Science can be a good career choice. It offers a range of career paths in IT sectors, such as software development, system administration, and computer network architecture.

Q6. Does Computer Science involve physics?
A6: Yes. The field of Computer Science includes Physics as a subject in the curriculum as several core Computing concepts rely on electromagnetic, quantum, mechanical and thermodynamic principles.

Q7. Does BSc Computer Science have coding?
A7: Yes. The core academic component of BSc Computer Science demands heavy coding involvement throughout the curriculum. Students will learn different programming languages that will help them to write code to solve issues and create software applications.
