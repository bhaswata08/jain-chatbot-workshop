FAQ's:
Q1. What is VFX and Animation?
A1: VFX is the process of adding computer-generated imagery to live-action footage to create scenes that couldn't be filmed realistically. On the other hand, Animation is the technique of creating moving images from scratch by manipulating drawings or 3D models.

Q2. Is MA VFX and Animation a good career?
A2: Yes. An MA in VFX and Animation can be a good career choice, as the demand for skilled VFX and Animation professionals is high across industries such as film, television, advertising, and gaming.

Q3. What is the full form of VFX?
A3: The full form of VFX stands for Visual Effects.

A5: No. Coding is not required to create animations, but it can be helpful. Animators with coding knowledge have an advantage, as Animation is an evolved form of coding.
