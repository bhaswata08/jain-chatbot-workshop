FAQ's:
Q1: Is BJMC a good career option?
A1: Yes. A Bachelor of Journalism and Mass Communication (BJMC) degree can be a good career option for students interested in journalism, media, public relations, advertising and communication.

Q2: Who is the father of Journalism?
A2: James Augustus Hickey is known as the father of Indian journalism. He started the first newspaper in India, the Bengal Gazette, in 1780.

Q3: Which is the highest-paid job in Mass Communication?
A3: Amidst different promising career avenues, a corporate communications manager or public relations manager is one of the highest-paid jobs in the field of mass communication. These roles involve managing a company's public image and communication strategies, often commanding high salaries due to their strategic importance.

Q4: Is Journalism a good job?
A4: With the growing media and entertainment industry, journalism offers great career prospects for students in India and abroad.

Q5: What is the scope of Journalism?
A5: The scope of journalism is broad and includes gathering, analysing, and sharing news and information with the public. It's a growing field where students need to cover a broad range of topics and issues.

Q6: Is Journalism a high-paying job?
A6: The salary for a journalist in India can vary widely depending on experience, location, and type of job. Journalists who specialise in niche areas like finance, technology, or healthcare tend to earn more.

Q7: Is Journalism hard to study?
A7: Most journalists have to switch between a number of stories at once which all have different deadlines - this means that there is a focus and time management that has to be executed well. Because of this, many journalists struggle with using all of the skills they have or need to report including sophisticated writing, complex problem solving, ethical journalism, pacing, and many more in an ever-changing field of media.

Q10: Is Journalism a government job?
A10: In most countries, journalism is meant to be independent of government influence. Therefore, government jobs in this sector may be very limited or restricted. However, some government agencies may employ journalists for public relations or communications roles.

Q11: Is Journalism easy to study?
A11: Studying journalism is neither easy nor difficult. It depends on the student's interests and skills. Success in this field requires a passion for storytelling and ethical reporting. Students must also be willing and daring enough to adapt to the ever-changing media landscape.

Q12: Can I do an MBA after a BA in Journalism?
A12: Yes. Students can pursue an MBA after completing a BA in Journalism. Nonetheless, every university or business school has its own criteria. Students should carry out research to ascertain that they meet the requirements.

Q13: What is the future of Mass Communication?
A13: The future of mass communication is evolving with the shift to digital media, the rise of personalised content, and the integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Q14: How many years can I study Mass Communication?
A14: Generally speaking, students can study Mass Communication for three years to complete a Bachelor's degree in Mass Communication, followed by a two-year Master's degree in the same or related field.

Q15: What is the scope of Mass Communication?
A15: Mass communication is a broad field that involves creating, distributing, and analysing information and messages to a large audience. It includes journalism, advertising, public relations, and more.
