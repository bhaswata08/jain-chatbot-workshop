FAQ's:
Q1. What is meant by interaction design?
A1: Interaction design is the process of creating interfaces that allow users to interact with products and services in a way that feels human-like.

Q2. Is Interaction Design the same as UX Design?
A2: Interaction design (IxD) is a part of user experience (UX) design, but the two are not the same. IxD is concerned with making the product respond to user actions appropriately. It acts as a bridge between the user interface (UI) and the user.

Q3. What is UI and Interactive Design?
A3: User interface (UI) design focuses on the look and feel of a product, while interaction design focuses on how a user interacts with that product. Both are part of user experience (UX) design.

Q4. What is the goal of Interaction Design?
A4: The goal of interaction design is to create products that will help the users accomplish their end goals in the best way possible.

Q5. What are the 5 dimensions of Interaction Design?
A5: The five dimensions of interaction design are words, visual representations, physical objects/space, time, and behaviour.
