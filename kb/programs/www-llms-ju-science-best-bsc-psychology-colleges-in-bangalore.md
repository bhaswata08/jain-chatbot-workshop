FAQ's:
Q1. Is Psychology a good career?
A1: Yes. Psychology can be a good career choice if students are passionate about understanding human behaviour, want to make a positive impact on people's lives, and are comfortable working with individuals facing mental health challenges.

Q2. Is Psychology hard to study?
A2: No. Studying Psychology is generally not considered "easy" as it requires critical thinking, analysing complex theories, and understanding diverse human behaviour.

Q3. Can Psychologists work in hospitals?
A3: Yes. Psychologists can work in hospitals. They can provide therapy and counselling services to the patients and develop treatment plans that consider their both mental and physical health needs.

Q4. What are the subjects in BSc Psychology?
A4: The BSc Psychology subjects include: Introduction to Psychology, Biological Psychology, Cognitive Psychology, Developmental Psychology, Social Psychology, Abnormal Psychology, Personality Psychology, and Research Methods in Psychology.

Q5. How many years is BSc Psychology?
A5: A Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Psychology is generally a three-year undergraduate course.

Q6. Is there Maths in BSc Psychology?
A6: Yes. A BSc in Psychology includes Maths, which is often used in Psychology to model psychological theories and experimental phenomena.

Q7. Does BSc Psychology require NEET?
A7: No. NEET is not required to pursue a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Psychology. NEET is an entrance exam for medical and dental courses in India.

Q8. What is the salary of BSc Psychology?
A8: The average salary for a BSc in Psychology graduate in India can range from INR 2.5 LPA to 6 LPA.

Q9. Does BSc Psychology have scope?
A9: Yes. A Bachelor of Science in Psychology has a wide range of career paths. It can lead to jobs in research, counselling, education, and more.

Q10. Can I get a job after BSc Psychology?
A10: Yes. Students can work in different areas such as counselling, research, education, human resources, social work, and even marketing. Through further specialisation and experience, students can aim for more advanced positions in clinical psychology or related fields.

Q11. Can I become a Psychiatrist after BSc Psychology?
A11: No. A BSc in Psychology alone is not enough to become a psychiatrist. To become a psychiatrist, students must attend medical school and specialise in psychiatry.
