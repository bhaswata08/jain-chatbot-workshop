FAQ's:
Q1. Does UI/UX require coding?
A1: No. Coding is not required for a UI/UX designer. UI/UX designers focus on analysing users, creating layouts, and designing visual systems which are not dependent on coding.

Q2. Is UX Design harder than coding?
A2: Whether UX design or coding is harder depends on the student's strengths, weaknesses, and interests. Both fields require different skill sets and mindsets. Some may find the creative and user-focused aspects of UX design more challenging, while others may struggle more with the logical and technical demands of coding.

Q3. Is UX Design high paying?
A3: Yes. UX Design can be a high-paying career. A Glassdoor study ranks UX Design as the sixth highest-paying job at the entry-level.

Q4. Is UI/UX Design a good career?
A4: Yes. UI/UX design is considered a good career choice due to high demand across industries, the potential for a competitive salary, and provides a fulfilling career path for those passionate about user-centric design and digital products.

Q5. Is UI/UX in demand in India?
A5: Yes. UI/UX design is in high demand in India across various fields, mainly in start-ups, healthcare (telemedicine), tech industries, and e-Commerce. This field is expected to continue to grow as the country's digital sector expands.
