FAQ's:
Q1: Is an MA in Economics tough?
A1: An MA in Economics can be challenging due to many reasons. It requires a high level of mathematical skills and the ability to assess and evaluate data that are of economic value.

Q2: Does MA Economics have Maths?
A2: Yes. An MA in Economics typically involves a significant amount of mathematics. The curriculum covers topics like calculus, linear algebra, statistics, and probability theory to understand and analyse economic models and data effectively.

Q3: Is Economics a good career?
A3: Yes. A career in economics offers many opportunities across sectors like finance, consulting, government, academia, and research.

Q4: Is an MA in Economics worth it?
A4: Yes. An MA in Economics can help students advance their careers, increase their salary range, and gain specialised skills. They can work in economic forecasting, policy analysis, market research, and data analytics across consulting firms, financial institutions, government agencies, and research organisations.

Q5: What is the scope of an MA in Economics?
A5: An MA in Economics can lead to a variety of careers in sectors like banking, finance, and business management.

Q6: Can I do PhD after my MA in Economics?
A6: Yes. To pursue most doctoral studies, students ought to have a Master's degree first.
