FAQ's:
Q1. Is a Master's in Digital Business worth it?
A1: A Master's in Digital Business is an ideal choice for those interested in learning about technology as part of digital transformation. The curriculum includes subjects such as Digital Marketing, E-Commerce, Data Analytics, and Business Strategy. Exposure to these key areas of business makes graduates qualified for a variety of different sectors and job profiles.

Q2. Is Digital Business in demand?
A2: Yes. The shift towards online platforms and the need for businesses to reach customers digitally has fuelled the demand for professionals with digital business skills.

Q3. Can I get a job after MBA in Digital Marketing?
A3: Yes. You can get a job after an MBA in Digital Marketing. The degree equips professionals with the skills to leverage digital strategies effectively, using social media platforms and traditional marketing channels to build strong brand strategies. With the growing online presence of customers, businesses across industries are actively hiring digital marketers to establish their market position.

Q4. Is Digital Business a good degree?
A4: Yes. It is a good course that offers job roles in marketing, advertising, and brand management. Graduates can also consider pursuing specific value-added certifications as well as further studies to improve their overall profile.

Q5. Is Digital Business the future?
A5: Embracing strategies for Digital Business is vital to a business's future, for digital transformation is reshaping businesses, reevaluating customer experiences, and also driving revolutionary models. Several businesses who fail to change risk falling behind. Multiple companies that embrace technology can unlock new opportunities, drive growth, and stay competitive.

Q6. What is the scope of Digital Business?
A6: The scope of Digital Business includes using technology to develop additional value chains, business opportunities, and customer experiences, all while transforming how businesses operate and interact with customers, employees, and even partners.
