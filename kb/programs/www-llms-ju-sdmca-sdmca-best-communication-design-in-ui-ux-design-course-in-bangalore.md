FAQ's:
Q1. Is UI/UX a good career?
A1: Yes. UI/UX design is considered a good career choice due to the high demand to create user-friendly interfaces across websites, apps, and other platforms. Salaries for UI/UX designers are generally competitive, with opportunities for career progression and higher pay with experience.

Q2. Does UX Design require Math?
A2: Yes. UX Design involves mathematical concepts, though not in a complex way. Fundamental design techniques, such as proportions, symmetry, and spatial relationships, are rooted in math and are applied intuitively by designers.

Q3. What is UI/UX Designer salary?
A3: In India, the salary of a UI/UX designer can range from INR 3 lakhs to INR 22 LPA, depending on experience and role.

Q4. Does UX Design require coding?
A4: No. UX designers focus on the design of a product, while developers are responsible for coding.

Q5. Is UI/UX in high demand?
A5: Businesses are realising the value of creating intuitive, user-friendly products and services. Therefore, UI/UX design is in high demand, and it's expected to remain so as businesses continue to digitise.

Q6. Will AI replace UI/UX Designers?
A6: No. AI is highly unlikely to completely replace UI/UX designers. While AI can automate certain tasks and assist in the design process, it currently lacks the crucial human qualities like empathy, creativity, and complex problem-solving needed to fully design user experiences effectively.

Q7. Does UX Design have a future?
A7: Yes. UX design has a future, with a growing demand for skilled professionals. New technologies like voice interfaces, VR, and AR are creating new opportunities for UX designers.

Q8. Is UI/UX Front-end?
A8: UI is related to the Front-End as it involves the visual design and layout of Front-End components. However, UX goes beyond just the Front-End by focusing on the overall user experience, including usability, functionality, and user interaction.

Q9. Is UI/UX an IT job?
A9: Yes. UI/UX design is an IT job role that involves working on digital products. However, UI/UX designers are in high demand across different industries and are not limited to exposure to IT sectors.
