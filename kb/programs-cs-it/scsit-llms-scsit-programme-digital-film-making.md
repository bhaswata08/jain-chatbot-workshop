FAQ's:
Q1: What is a Film degree called?
A1: A Film degree is typically called a Bachelor of Arts (BA) in Film Studies or a Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Filmmaking. Some universities like JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) also offer a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Digital Filmmaking which focuses on the practical knowledge and technical skills for creating professional quality digital films.

Q2: What is the future of Filmmaking?
A2: As digital technology and AI-driven tools continue transforming the film industry, the future of filmmaking looks promising. In addition to this, the advancements in streaming platforms and immersive storytelling are also creating new opportunities for filmmakers worldwide.

Q3: When did Digital Filmmaking start?
A3: Digital filmmaking started in the 1980s, but it became popular and common during the early 2000s. 'Windhorse' is considered by most of the sources as one of the first feature-length films shot and post-produced through a digital filmmaking process. Windhorse was created in 1996.

Q4: Is Filmmaking a good career?
A4: Yes. Filmmaking is a good and rewarding career for those passionate about storytelling, creativity, and visual arts. It offers diverse opportunities in directing, editing, cinematography, and screenwriting. However, individuals must know that the field of filmmaking is also highly competitive.
