Q3: What is IoT in simple words?
A3: IoT, or the Internet of Things, is a network of different devices that stay connected and share data over the Internet. A smart AC controlled through a phone or a smartwatch that counts the number of steps are a few examples of IoT.

Q4: What is the definition of IoT?
A4: IoT, or the Internet of Things, is a network of multiple devices. The various devices in IoT stay connected with each other. The IoT devices are able to communicate mutually without any human help or interference.

Q5: What is the use of IoT?
A5: IoT makes life easier and simpler by connecting devices and automating various tasks. Wearable tech like smartwatches and smart homes with remote control of AC and lighting are some of the best examples of IoT.

Q6: Why is IoT important?
A6: IoT saves time and effort by automating different tasks and improving efficiency. It makes life easier through smart and connected devices that do not require human intervention. IoT also enhances safety and healthcare.
