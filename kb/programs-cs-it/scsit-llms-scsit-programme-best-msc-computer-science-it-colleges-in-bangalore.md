Q1: How many years is it to get an MSc in Computer Science?
A1: The degree of MSc Computer Science requires two years to complete. The duration of the degree may vary depending on the university and country, as a few offer a one-year accelerated programme.

Q2: Is MSc Computer Science equivalent to MCA?
A2: The degree of MSc Computer Science differs from the MCA degree. The MSc Computer Science students study computer science theory and research opportunities, while MCA students mainly study software development and information technology.

Q3: Is an MSc in Computer Science worth it?
A3: Yes. An MSc in Computer Science is worth it because it offers in-depth computer science knowledge and diverse research opportunities. The degree holders of MSc in Computer Science get promising job opportunities with lucrative salary packages.

Q4: What is the scope of MSc IT?
A4: The scope of the MSc IT degree is quite extensive. MSc IT degree holders can achieve significant job roles in software development, networking, cybersecurity, data analytics, etc. Graduates can explore job opportunities in IT firms and pursue PhD programmes.
