FAQ's:
Q1: What is an example of visual communication?
A1: An infographic representing data in an attractive and clear manner is a good example of visual communication. Some other examples of visual communication include posters, advertisements, videos and animations.

Q2: What are the advantages of visual communication?
A2: Visual communication provides information in a quick, easy and attractive way. It is more interesting than text alone and represents ideas effectively. Due to these advantages, visual communication is immensely helpful in the fields of marketing, media and communication.

Q3: What is BSc Visual Communication?
A3: BSc (Bachelor of Science) Visual Communication is a three-year undergraduate degree programme that focuses on designing and delivering visual content. Students of this programme learn about various media for delivering visual content. The programme covers a wide range of topics like animation, graphic design, photography, and digital media. The graduate of BSc in Visual Communication can pursue creative careers in communication and design.

Q4: What is the scope of BSc Visual Communication?
A4: The degree of BSc Visual Communication unlocks doors of diverse career opportunities in advertising, media, graphic design, animation, filmmaking, and digital marketing. Graduates can opt for job roles like designers, video editors, animators, or content creators.
