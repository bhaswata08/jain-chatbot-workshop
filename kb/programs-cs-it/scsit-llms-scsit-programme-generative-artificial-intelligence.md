FAQ's:
Q1: Is Generative AI the future?
A1: Generative AI is expected to play a significant role in the future, especially in content creation, automation, and problem-solving across industries.

Q2: What are the types of Generative AI?
A2: The main types of Generative AI include text generation (like ChatGPT), image generation, video generation, music composition, and code generation.

Q3: What is the difference between Generative AI and General AI?
A3: Generative AI focuses on creating content based on patterns in data, while General AI aims to think and reason like a human across different tasks.

Q4: Why is it called Generative AI?
A4: It is called Generative AI because it generates new content. For example, text, images, or music, based on learned data patterns.

Q5: Will Generative AI replace humans?
A5: Generative AI can automate many tasks, but human creativity and intelligence are irreplaceable. Human judgment and decision-making will still be essential in many fields.
