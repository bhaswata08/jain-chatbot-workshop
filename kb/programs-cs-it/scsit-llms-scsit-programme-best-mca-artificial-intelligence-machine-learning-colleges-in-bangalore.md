FAQ's:
Q1: Does AI require coding?
A1: Yes. AI requires coding, as candidates have to develop Machine Learning models and automate processes. These tasks involve knowledge of coding. Coding languages like Python, R, Java, etc., are also crucial to AI.

Q2: What is AI engineering salary?
A2: In India, entry-level AI Engineers earn around ₹6-10 LPA, while experienced professionals can earn ₹20 LPA or more. Globally, salaries are higher, especially in countries with a strong tech industry.

Q3: Is Artificial Intelligence expensive?
A3: Yes. Artificial Intelligence can be expensive due to the high costs of data processing, computing power, and skilled professionals.

Q4: Who is eligible for the AI course?
A4: Anyone with a Mathematics, programming, or Computer Science background can pursue an AI course. Many courses are also available for beginners. However, advanced courses like MCA in AI require candidates to have passed grade 12th with Mathematics as the core subject and bachelor's degree, with at least 50% marks.
