### Student Conduct Policy

###### Discipline

Misuse or damage to University property or facilities will lead to disciplinary action which could result in termination of a student's studies and / or payment for damages and call out charges. Breach of discipline can range from a failure to comply with any statute, ordinance, regulation, or rule prescribed for the conduct.

###### Security

Students will be responsible for their personal property. The University will not accept any responsibility for items lost or stolen. Items lost or found should be intimated at the facilities management desk.

###### Attendance Requirement

a) Undergraduate Programs (other than Engineering)
                        - A student is eligible to appear for the semester-end examinations, only if he / she has put in a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject.
- No candidate is permitted to take the end-semester examination if he / she does not fulfil the attendance requirement.
- If a student is absent from all the classes without prior permission, his / her name will be removed from the rolls of the University.

(b) Undergraduate Programs (Engineering)
- A student should have attended a minimum of 85% of the classes conducted in each subject. In case a student fails to fulfil the 85% attendance requirement, then the Dean / Director can consider condonation to a maximum extent of 10% provided the candidate has a minimum of 75% attendance in that semester.
- No candidate is permitted to take the end-semester examination if he / she does not fulfil the attendance requirement.
- If a student is absent from all the classes without prior permission, his / her name will be removed from the rolls of the University.

The above requirements may strictly be adhered to.

The student failing to comply with attendance requirements shall be required to seek re-admission into the same semester by paying the stipulated fees.
