The library is the center of academic life and provides a range of services to support and enhance student learning at the campus. A team of subject specialist librarians and other trained staff provide a wide range of services to support their information needs and help them to make the most of the resources available.

- Study place for more than 200 readers
- Collection of printed books and journals
- A collection of 1,19,138 books as well as CDs and DVDs
- Online research journals
- 1,218 national and international periodicals
- Website listing thousands of sites grouped by subject
- PCs and laptops to access the database and the internet
- Access to other academic libraries in the City
- Self-service units for borrowing / returning library books
- A wide range of quality electronic library resource
- Online reading list

- Access all e-books and browse the content immediately
- Find exactly what you are looking for with a robust searching mechanism
- Place the most frequently accessed book titles on your bookshelf
- Facilitates easy purchase of the books after you have had the opportunity to browse it online
