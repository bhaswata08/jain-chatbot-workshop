National Cadet Corps is a leadership training activity for students that teaches them responsibility and resourcefulness. It also helps students to develop smart thinking, quick reasoning, and reaction skills and handle various situations. Those who are physically fit, are encouraged to join the NCC (National Cadet Corps).

The motto of NSS’s "Not Me But You ‘’reflects the essence of democratic living and to uphold the need for self-less service. NSS helps students to develop appreciation for another person's point of view and also shows consideration towards other living beings. The University encourages students to volunteer and be a part of this social wing.

The student organization of School of Commerce Studies is involved in real-time projects in the fields of marketing, HRM, Finance, R&D, and the like. Besides identifying and taking up sustainable projects, they have joined hands with several NGOs and are involved in activities to help those who are less fortunate. Enactus, through its entrepreneurial approach, has created a social, economic, and environmental impact. They aim at improving the quality of life and standard of living with the various programs for the communities.

Rotaract, the student wing of the Rotary Club is engaged in making students socially responsible through community and club services. There are around 150 student members involved in community service like mega blood donation camps, awareness programs, and medical camps. They also conduct skill development and orientation programs, leadership training workshops, awareness on health and hygiene, tree plantation, Swacch Bharat Abhiyaan, celebrate national festivals, and many more.

The Elite Club caters to the needs of the students by giving equal importance to extra-curricular activities. With a range of activities, the Club meets the communication needs of the students. The events include competitions, guest lectures, and activities such as debate, creative writing, word games and play held round the year. The literary activities of the Club enable students to flourish in both verbal and non-verbal pattern of communications.

Hindi Parishad creates an awareness and interest towards the language. The Club encourages and provides a platform for students to conduct and participate in inter-collegiate competitions and various literary activities that celebrate Hindi literature. Guest lectures, Guru Purnima, and Hindi Diwas are some of the important events celebrated by the Club.

Kannada Vedike at JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) creates awareness among students about the Kannada language, rich culture of Karnataka, its history and heritage. Patriotism towards Kannada is celebrated through this Club. Kannada Vedike also conducts intra-collegiate fest during which students come together and perform cultural programs to promote Kannada literature and culture.

The Cultural Forum is the driving force behind organizing all the cultural fests of the University. The Forum provides a platform for individuals to showcase their talent in the events conducted by other institutions.

The Commerce Forum is an integrated part of the Commerce Department. It gives students an opportunity to become young professionals and develop in them a feeling of team spirit. The qualities of a true leader is to set standards for others to follow and the Forum has been instrumental in bringing out the hidden talents and abilities of every student.

The Science Forum believes in bringing out the creative expression of scientific temper in an individual making science as an interesting subject. It conducts various inter-class and inter-collegiate activities under the guidance of coordinators and the members.
