The CMS Festival is an amalgamation of fests under the JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) - Center for Management Studies. The festivals together combine education, fun, and explores different facets of a human being that complement one another - the professional, the aesthetic, and personal excellence.

Cranium, the Management Fest of CMS Business School, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) is one of the most admired platforms for management students to exhibit and showcase their capabilities, talents, and strengths. It is the journey of exploring and discovering deep into the sea of administration, business, entrepreneurship, and management where only the best brains can sail across the storms and tides of the real business world challenges.

The Infinity annual intra-university cultural festival of School of Engineering & Technology is spread over three days. This mega in-house event is held branch-wise with events covering cultural, technical, and management fields.

Samanvaya is a national-level business fest conducted by JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) - School of Commerce & Management Studies. It has set trends throughout the years and has been at the forefront of conducting innovative events and attracting the best participants from all over India. This has been a nurturing ground for talent and ideas, and the trend continues to grow year by year.

Taru Samskriti is an annual all India inter-collegiate techno - comm- cultural fest that provides a platform for students across the country to showcase their talents. This event, over the past years, has witnessed excellent country-wide participation bringing great acclaim and appreciation.

The expo is a real-time experience that connects students to the products and services marketed by corporates to the real world. This helps in encouraging students learn from practical business scenarios and introducing them to the concept of technical presentations to build marketing skills and help in model building.

It is an attempt to encourage hands on experience of creating innovative and exploratory models in science which will help the students concretize some of the concepts of science that they learn during their course of study. This outreach program invites other Universities to participate in the exhibition with their exhibits / models.
