### Health & Wellbeing

###### Human Networking Academy (HNA)

The faculty of Human Networking Academy, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) conducts a unique, innovative, and experiential course which addresses the need to equip students with requisite knowledge and skills to strengthen their mind. The motive behind conducting this course is to create an individual who is intellectually alert, morally upright, and physically strong. Awareness about our national history, culture, social responsibilities, and human values is created through interactive sessions; short, educational, and theme-based films; University documentaries; and outbound learning programs.

###### Health care

The University provides quality medical and mental health care on the campuses. Vishwas (counseling center) offers psychological counseling and psychiatric services to students to cope with personal problems and assist them to successfully achieve their educational goals. The services include health examination, immunization and health history, health care wellness services, mental health services, special care to students with disabilities, and awareness programs.
