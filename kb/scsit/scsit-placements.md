### Placements

JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), School of Computer Science and Information Technology provides the platform for the students to think and realise their dreams. With the excellent Industry and Academic interface, the department has been setting new benchmarks in campus placements across different domains of computer science and related fields. The department has been a consistent topper in the list of top recruiters for campus placements across the globe.

With an impeccable placement record of up to 307+ students placed in over 215+ companies, we remain staunch in our commitment towards providing the students with the most remunerative career opportunities.  Our highest salary package is 22 LPA while the average salary package is around 4.7 LPA across all the courses.
