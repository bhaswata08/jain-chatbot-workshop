The JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), School of Computer Science & Information Technology strives to bridge the gap between the Curriculum, Industry and Research. The courses are designed to develop the students into skilled professionals with practical and fundamental knowledge of technology and digital space.

Our career-focused undergraduate courses are designed to get you ready for the modern workplace. We offer flexibility in courses so that you can choose them according to your interests and career ambitions.

The post graduate courses are designed to enhance the existing skills and helps you to take your academic interest to the next level and support your career aspirations.

- Information Security Integrated with the Certification In CompTIA Security +
- Storage and Cloud Technology integrated with Certification in CompTIA Cloud+
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

The doctoral courses emphasise on the interdisciplinary research and innovation that strives to cultivate a strong culture of inter-and intra-institution research collaborations with the leading research institutes.

Overview

We offer the traditional PhD research degree that allows you to complete a piece of original research with expert guidance from world-class academics.
