JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) offers students courses and opportunities to apply their understanding of the subjects to practical situations.

The University provides a wide range of undergraduate programs with a focus on relevance, application, and practicality. The emphasis is also on laying a strong foundation for students to help them gain knowledge in their chosen field. With experienced faculty and state-of-the-art facilities, students are exposed to ideas and activities that will encourage them to take up specialized areas of study.

JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) offers 58 undergraduate courses out of which 33 are in innovative/emerging areas.

The University provides financial aid to the needy and eligible students. Merit scholarships for the meritorious students are provided every academic year. Fee concession is provided for economically weaker students and students belonging to disadvantaged groups. A special test for merit scholarship is conducted for the management students and scholarships are awarded. The eligibility for taking the test is 80% in the qualifying exam. Merit scholarships are given to students as an incentive for improving their performance. Sports scholarships are given to outstanding sports persons in the form of fee concessions, cash incentives, sponsorships to tournaments, etc.
