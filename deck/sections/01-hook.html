Act 1 / Fact check

Count the inventions

The percentage

"40% refunded."

JAIN's page says 50% is deducted for this case.

The money

₹2,50,000 tuition, ₹5,000 + ₹2,000 fees.

None of these numbers appear in JAIN's refund rules.

The email

finance@jainuniversity.edu

Not on any page we collected. A student could send documents to it.

Tables, steps, tips. All fluent. Mostly made up.

Act 1 / The real answer

What JAIN's own page says

When you ask for a refundDeduction
More than 15 days before the last date5% (max ₹5,000)
Less than 15 days before10%
Up to 15 days after20%
16–30 days after50%
More than 30 days afterNo refund

20 days after → you lose half the fee. BCA/MCA last date: 20 July 2026.

Act 1 / Two causes

Where the wrong answer came from

It never saw the page

JAIN's 2026 refund table is not in its training data, or is buried under thousands of other universities' rules.

Training data also stops at a cutoff date.

It is trained to sound sure

"I don't know" is rarely the most likely next text. A fluent guess is.

This is called hallucination.
