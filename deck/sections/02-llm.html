Act 2 / Three knobs you will touch today

Words you will see in the lab

Context window

Everything the model can "see" at once: your prompt, the history, the documents.

Not memory. When it is full, old text falls off.

Temperature

0 = always pick the top token. Higher = more random, more creative.

Facts bot → keep it low.

Max tokens

A cap on how long the reply can be.

Too low and answers get cut mid-sentence.
Act 2 / Temperature

Temperature: how random is the next token?

0 = always the top token. Higher = more random. Facts bot → keep it low.

Act 2 / A common misconception

It is not a calculator. Or a database.

It does not look up anything. It does not compute anything.

It produces text that is likely to come next.

Sometimes likely text is the true answer. Sometimes it is not.
