Act 5 / Who picks?

Two ways to find the right page

Ask a smaller LLM

"Here are 129 page titles. Which ones answer this question?"

An extra LLM call every time. Titles hide the content. It can guess wrong too.

Embeddings

Turn every page and the question into numbers. Pick the closest.

Fast, cheap, no LLM in the loop.

Prompting the picker is prompt engineering. We'll get to it at the end.

Act 5 / Chunking

One vector per document is too coarse

Too long

Our NEP page is 9,705 words.

One vector has to average all of it.

Mixed topics

A programme page covers fees, syllabus, eligibility, placements.

Its vector points at none of them clearly.

Dead weight

Menus, footers, boilerplate.

Retrieved along with the one useful paragraph.

So split documents into sub-documents: chunks.

kb/academics/www-academics-nep.md, wc -w

Act 5 / Chunking

Chunks live inside the store

Embed and search sub-documents, not whole pages.

Act 5 / Chunking

Three ways to cut

Fixed size

Every N tokens, with some overlap.

Fees

Hostel: ₹1 lakh more.
Attendance

only with 75% attendance.
Chunk 2 alone: "you can sit the exam."

Recursive

Split on headings, then paragraphs, then sentences, until it fits.

Hostel: ₹1 lakh more.
heading
Attendance
You can sit the semester-end exam
only with 75% attendance.
The usual default.

Semantic

Cut where the meaning shifts, using embeddings.

Fees
Tuition: ₹2.5 lakh a year.0.82
Hostel: ₹1 lakh more.0.19
meaning shifts
Attendance

only with 75% attendance.
Best chunks, slowest to build.

Attendance rule: jainuniversity.ac.in · Student conduct policy · fetched 5 Nov 2026

The fixed-size cut lands between "on" and "ly". Ask the bot "Can I write the exam with 60% attendance?" and if only chunk 2 comes back, the answer is yes. The real rule (75% minimum, condonation down from 85%) is in chunk 3. Recursive and semantic keep the sentence whole.
Act 5 / Vector store

The vectors need a home

Chunks plus their vectors, stored and searchable.

Act 5 / Vector store

Where do the vectors live?

A NumPy array

Fine for a handful of documents. Honestly, use it.

At thousands: how do you update one page? Delete ten? Filter by category? Save it?

A vector store

A database built for vectors: add, update, delete by id, filter by metadata, fast search.

Chroma, Qdrant, pgvector, LanceDB

Same idea as any database: CRUD, just for vectors.

Act 5 / What still goes wrong

RAG is not magic

Wrong chunk

Search returns the PhD refund table for a BCA question.

The answer is fluent and wrong again.

Not in the pages

"Will the HOD sign my attendance form if I look really sad?"

Without the "I don't know" rule, it says yes.

Stale data

Our snapshot is from 5 Nov 2026.

Fees change. Re-run ingestion.

RAG doesn't eliminate stupidity. It gives stupidity better references.

On the HOD one: "The bot will confidently say yes, and we will not bail you out." Then: always show the source so a human can check.
